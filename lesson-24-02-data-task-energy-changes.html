📘 The Lesson: Reading Energy Changes
Every system tells a story. Find the proof.
⏱ 15 min

Part 1 · Every System Has a Starting and a Resulting Energy

A flashlight is a systemSystemParts working together.. The starting energyStarting energyWhat's there at the beginning. is the electrical energy stored in the battery. The resulting energyResulting energyWhat's there at the end. is light. That's the energy changeEnergy changeOne form becomes another..

Almost everything around you has a starting and resulting energy. A toaster takes electrical → heat. A wind turbine takes motion → electrical. Your body takes food (chemical) → motion (and heat too). Once you spot the pattern, you start seeing it everywhere.

In Florida: sunshine starts lots of energy changes. Solar panels turn light into electrical energy, leaves turn it into food (chemical energy), and dark roads turn it into heat you can feel through your shoes.

Part 2 · Evidence Is What Backs Up Your Claim

When you say "this is electrical → light," that's a claimClaimA statement that needs proof.. EvidenceEvidenceObserved proof. is what makes the claim believable. Evidence might be:

  • What you SAW: the bulb glowed; the toast turned brown.
  • What you HEARD: the speaker buzzed; the doorbell rang.
  • What you FELT: the spoon warmed up; the breeze got stronger.
  • What you MEASURED: the temperature went up; the pinwheel made 10 spins in 5 seconds.

A great claim names the energy change AND points to the proof. Example: "This flashlight is electrical → light energy. My evidence is that when I push the button, the battery’s electricity flows and light comes out the front."

Watch out: some people think energy "appears" in a system or "disappears." It doesn't. It changes FORM. The starting energy was always there; it just changes into the resulting energy. You can usually trace where it came from.

Quick Self-Check
Not graded — just for you!
Q1

A toaster turns on. Bread goes from cool to hot and brown. What was the STARTING energy?

AHeat energy from the bread
BElectrical energy from the wall outlet
CChemical energy from the bread itself
🟢 Right! The starting energy is the electricity going into the toaster — without that, no heat would happen.
The HEAT and brown bread are the RESULTING energy. The starting energy came from the OUTLET — electrical energy.
Q2

Which is the BEST piece of evidence that a flashlight converted electrical energy into light?

AWhen you click the button, the bulb glows
BThe flashlight is heavy
CIt is made of plastic
🔍 Right! The glow is the observable proof that light energy is now there. That's your evidence.
Weight or material isn't evidence of an energy change. The GLOW is what proves light was produced.
Q3

You pedal a bike fast. The wheels spin and a generator on the wheels lights a tiny bulb. List the energy changes.

ALight → motion → chemical
BSound → electrical → heat
CChemical (food) → motion → electrical → light
↪️ Right! Food gave you chemical energy that became motion, the generator turned motion into electrical, and the bulb turned electrical into light.
Trace it through. You ate FOOD (chemical), which let your legs MOVE (motion), the generator made ELECTRICITY (electrical), and the bulb made LIGHT.
