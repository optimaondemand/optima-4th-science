📘 The Lesson: When Nature Acts Big
Three layers of effect — land, ecosystem, people
⏱ 15 min

Part 1 · Cause and Effect, Step by Step

Florida is shaped by big natural eventsNatural eventSomething big that happens in nature.: hurricanes, floods, wildfires, sinkholes and freezes. When one happens, scientists trace its effects with a simple idea: causeCauseWhat makes something happen. → effectEffectA change caused by something.. The event is the cause. Everything that changes because of it is an effect.

Not every natural event is a hazardHazardA natural event that can cause harm.. A summer thunderstorm is normal Florida weather. A strong hurricane coming on shore is a hazard. The difference is whether the event can harm people, animals or the things people own.

Part 2 · Three Layers to Trace

When scientists study a Florida hazard, they look at effects in three layers:

  • The LAND. Soil washes away. Beaches lose sand. Trees fall. Sinkholes open. Roads break.
  • The ecosystemEcosystemLiving and nonliving things in one place.. Animals lose homes. Saltwater pushes inland and damages fresh-water plants. Fires clear out low bushes, and some plants need that. The web of life shifts.
  • The PEOPLE. Homes flood. Power goes out. Schools close. Families evacuateEvacuateLeave a dangerous area to stay safe.. Roads close for days. Sometimes lives are lost.

Each layer affects the others. Lost trees (LAND) means lost shelter for birds (ECOSYSTEM) and less shade for neighborhoods (PEOPLE). One event ripples outward in every direction.

🧲 Two Hidden Forces in Every Natural Event

Gravity is the pull that brings everything down toward Earth. It makes rain fall and floodwater rush downhill into low streets.

Friction is a force that holds things back when two surfaces rub. Tree roots grip the soil with friction. When heavy rain soaks the ground, that grip gets weaker, so wind and gravity can pull trees over.

Quick Self-Check
Not graded — just for you!
Q1

What's the difference between a CAUSE and an EFFECT?

AThey're the same thing
BA cause is what makes something happen; an effect is what changes because of it
CA cause is people; an effect is nature
🎯 Right! Cause makes it happen — effect is what changes because of it.
A cause is what makes something happen (heavy rain). An effect is what changes because of it (flooded streets). They're always connected — but different.
Q2

A Florida wildfire burns through a pine forest. Which of these is an effect on the ECOSYSTEM (not land or people)?

AAnimals like gopher tortoises lose their homes
BRoads close to traffic
CA house is damaged
🌎 Right! Animals losing habitat is an ECOSYSTEM effect.
Animals losing habitat is an ECOSYSTEM effect. Closed roads and damaged houses are effects on PEOPLE (and the LAND).
Q3

Which Florida natural event is caused by water slowly dissolving underground limestone over many years?

AHurricane
BWildfire
CSinkhole
🕳️ Right! Sinkholes form when underground limestone dissolves and the surface above collapses.
Hurricanes form over warm ocean water. Wildfires need dry plants and a spark. Sinkholes form when underground limestone slowly dissolves — Florida has more sinkholes than any other state.
