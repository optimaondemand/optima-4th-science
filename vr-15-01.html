Case File 15 · A supply order
LR
From Dr. Lena Reyes, field biologist

Only order what refills!

Our Field Station is opening a camp on a little island. It has to run for 20 years, and boats can’t bring supplies very often.

A volunteer filled out the supply order. Some lines are great. Some lines would run out and never come back. Help me plan a camp that keeps going.

♻️ Renewable: comes back on its own⛔ Nonrenewable: once it’s gone, it’s gone
FIELD STATION · SUPPLY ORDER No. 15-01

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your camp
    Your camp has three areas. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    ⚡ Shelter and powerOptima Domi Tent, Solar Panel with Stand, Water Wheel (Moving)type: Tent · Solar · Water WheelSunlight and moving water come back every day.

    🪵 Building and cookingLumber, Fire Pit, Fire Woodtype: Lumber · Fire Pit · Fire WoodTrees grow back, but only if we plant new ones.
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Island Town. (Typing Island in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. Find an open, sunny spot for your camp.
  5. Step 3 · Shelter and power
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Set up the Tent. Point the Solar Panel toward the open sky. Put the Water Wheel nearby (by water, if you can find some).
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Water, food and wood
    Add the Water Barrel, Corn, Sheep, Lumber, Fire Pit and Fire Wood around your tent. Then walk to each piece and say what it uses and why it won’t run out, like this:
    🗣 “The solar panel uses sunlight. The sun comes back every day, so it’s renewable.” · “The fire uses wood. It stays renewable only if we plant new trees.”
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyMake a little “leave it in the ground” pile away from camp: a Gold Nugget and a Granite Block. Say why the camp shouldn’t depend on them. (Hint: how long did they take to form?) Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see your whole camp. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 15.01 VR · Island Field Camp. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one piece of your camp and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

How-to video: Completing Assignments in Optima On-Demand (2 minutes)

These pictures come from a space map. Your map will be Island Town, but the buttons are the same.

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

  8. 8Pick the class and assignment

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs renewable nonrenewable refill or regrow a resource because
Try these sentence starters:

  • It lasted because ___.
  • When my camp used ___, it ran out in year ___.
  • That resource is nonrenewable, so ___.
  • Trees are renewable only if ___.
