0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know what to find
    These are the models you’ll bring out. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    🍽 FoodA deer (deer eat acorns)type: Deer
    🌼 PollinationA flower + a beetype: Flower Set, then Bee
    🏠 ShelterAn owltype: Barn Owl
    🍄 Nutrient cyclingFungus + bacteriatype: Fungal Cell, then Bacteria
    🤝 Bonus · human careA shoveltype: Shovel
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Tranquil Forest. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo.
  5. Step 3 · Pick your oak
    Look around and teleport to the biggest tree you can find. Stand right next to its trunk. This tree is Dr. Reyes’s oak.
  6. Step 4 · Build 4 connections
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Bring out each model and put it in its place:
    • 🍽 Food: the Deer next to the tree, where acorns fall.
    • 🌼 Pollination: a Flower Set near the tree, with the Bee right on a flower.
    • 🏠 Shelter: the Barn Owl up against the trunk, as high as you can reach.
    • 🍄 Nutrient cycling: the Fungal Cell and one Bacteria on the ground by the roots, where dead leaves rot.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller. Make the bee and the bacteria big enough to see!
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyAdd human care: put a Shovel next to a small tree nearby, as if someone just planted it. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 10.01 VR · Rebuild the Web. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Tell me you’re done
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. In the Canvas assignment box, type VR option submitted and turn it in.

Field Station stamp
Why one tree? A big live oak can live for hundreds of years. In that time, it becomes food, a home, and a compost pile for dozens of neighbors. Scientists call a living thing like this the center of the web.
VR field assistants earn the Golden Acorn badge. You’ll earn the Field Station stamp. Got a headset after all? Pick VR Field Mission at the top.
Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

The storm knocked the oak down. Pick two living things from your board. For each one, tell how it was connected to the oak and what changed for it when the oak fell. End with why one tree changed so much.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs two living things a kind of connection what changed why so much changed
Try these sentence starters:
  • The ___ was connected to the oak by ___. When the oak fell, ___.
  • The ___ used the oak for ___. Now it ___.
  • So much changed because ___.
