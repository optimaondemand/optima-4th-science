Case File 12 · A smudged dive slate
LR
From Dr. Lena Reyes, field biologist

The water smudged my sketches!

Remember the bat that hunted moths with echoes? On a dive in the Florida Keys, I watched a bottlenose dolphin hunt with echoes too! A hammerhead shark was hunting nearby, but it found its fish a different way.

I sketched both pathway models on my dive slate, but the water smudged them. Help me rebuild them side by side, so we can see what is the same and what is different.

  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Under The Ocean. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. You’ll be under the sea, just like on my dive!
  5. Step 3 · Place the brain
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Bring out the Brain Experience · Full Model, set it in an open spot and make it big. Both lanes will end here. (A real dolphin and a real shark each have their own brain. One model brain helps you compare them.)
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Build the two lanes
    Line up each lane so it points at the brain, with a gap between the two lanes:
    🐬 Striped Fish → blue arrow → Dolphin → blue arrow → brain🦈 Reef Fish → red arrow → Hammerhead → red arrow → brain
    Then stand at the fish end of each lane and say its four steps out loud, like this:
    🗣 “The click’s echo bounces off the fish (stimulus). The echo travels through the dolphin’s jaw to its ears (sense organ). Its brain works out where the fish is (brain). It swims over and grabs the fish (response).”
    Now say the shark’s lane in your own words. Hint: the shark smells a hurt fish.
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyAdd a third lane of your own. Pick any sea animal model (try the Green Sea Turtle), something it could notice, and two arrows to the brain. Say its four steps. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see both lanes and the brain. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 12.02 VR · Twin Pathways. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.

  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas

How-to video: Completing Assignments in Optima On-Demand (2 minutes)

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

  8. 8Pick the class and assignment

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.
