📘 The Lesson: Motion Carries Energy
More speed → more energy → bigger change
⏱ 15 min

Part 1 · Every Moving Object Carries Energy

EnergyEnergyThe ability to do work or cause change. is the ability to do work or cause change. Anything in motionMotionChanging position over time. is carrying a kind of energy called kinetic energyKinetic energyThe energy of motion.. A walking person, a falling raindrop and a rolling marble all have kinetic energy.

You can't see kinetic energy directly. But you can see what it does. A moving object can knock other objects over, make a sound, push something, or even break something. Those changes are evidenceEvidenceWhat you observe that supports a claim. that energy was there.

Part 2 · Speed Changes Everything

An object's speedSpeedHow fast something is moving. is how much distance it covers in a certain amount of time. A car going 30 miles in an hour is going 30 miles per hour. A snail going 1 inch in a minute is going 1 inch per minute. Same idea, very different speeds.

Here's the key rule for this lesson: the faster an object moves, the more energy it carries. A baseball thrown gently bumps into a target softly. The same baseball thrown hard hits the target with a CRACK. Same ball, different speed, different energy.

  • SLOWER object = less energy = smaller effect when it bumps something.
  • FASTER object = more energy = bigger effect.

Watch out: Some people think energy is only about how big or heavy something is. Size matters. But for two objects that are the same, the faster one ALWAYS has more energy. And speed counts a lot: a small baseball thrown fast can crack a window. A big beach ball rolling slowly just bumps it.

Part 3 · A Crash in Slow Motion

NASA scientists study crashes, too. They fire a small ball at a target at up to 15,000 miles per hour, then film it with a super-fast camera.

Quick Self-Check
Not graded — just for you!
Q1

What is KINETIC ENERGY?

AThe energy stored in food
BThe energy of motion — anything moving has it
CThe energy of sunlight
⚡ Right! Kinetic energy is the energy of motion — every moving object has it.
Food energy and sunlight are different kinds of energy. KINETIC energy is the energy of MOTION. Anything moving — a ball, a car, the wind — has it.
Q2

Two identical marbles roll across the floor. Marble A is twice as fast as marble B. Which has more kinetic energy?

AMarble A — the faster one
BMarble B — the slower one
CThey have the same energy
🏎️ Right! For the same object, faster always means more energy.
For two identical objects, the FASTER one always has more kinetic energy. That's the key relationship in this lesson.
Q3

You roll a ball at a cup. At a low speed, the cup barely moves. At a high speed, the cup flies across the table. What is the BEST scientific way to describe this evidence?

AThe cup got smarter
BThe ball changed weight
CA faster ball carried more energy, so the cup moved farther
🔍 Right! The DIFFERENT EFFECT (cup moving more) is the EVIDENCE for MORE ENERGY in the faster ball.
The ball didn't change weight, and cups don't get smarter! The faster ball carried more kinetic energy, so it caused a bigger change in the cup.
