📘 The Lesson: Energy Can Change Form
Devices convert input into output
⏱ 15 min

Part 1 · Transfer vs. Transformation

In the last few lessons, energy TRANSFERRED (moved) between things: one marble passed its motion to another, and sound traveled from a drum to your ear. Today’s idea is different. In transformationTransformationEnergy changes form., energy doesn’t just move. It CHANGES into a different form.

A toaster convertsConvertChange energy from one form to another. (changes) electrical energy INTO heat. A flashlight converts electrical energy INTO light.

Part 2 · Every Device Has Inputs and Outputs

A deviceDeviceA tool that converts energy. is a tool or machine built to do a job, and most devices convert energy. Every device has three parts to its energy story:

  • Input energyInput energyWhat goes in.: what goes IN. (A battery for a flashlight.)
  • The conversion: something inside the device that changes the form.
  • Output energyOutput energyWhat comes out.: what comes OUT, usually in a NEW form. (Light from the flashlight.)

The output you want is the useful energyUseful energyThe output you wanted.. But there are always extras. A flashlight makes light (useful) and also gets a little warm (extra heat). No device turns ALL its energy into the useful kind.

Watch out: Some people think a device USES UP energy, or MAKES new energy. It doesn’t do either. It only changes the form. Put 10 units of energy in, and 10 units come out, in different forms. Test it with the energy pipes.

Part 3 · Follow It Back to the Sun

Where did the toaster’s energy come from before it reached your kitchen? Follow it backward, one step at a time. Then follow one more device.

Quick Self-Check
Not graded — just for you!
Q1

What is energy CONVERSION (or transformation)?

AWhen energy gets used up
BWhen energy changes from one form into another
CWhen energy moves between two objects without changing
🔄 Right! Conversion means energy changes form (electric → light, motion → electric, etc.).
Energy doesn't get used up — and conversion isn't just movement. Conversion means the FORM of energy changes. A toaster converts electrical energy into heat.
Q2

A wind turbine takes in wind. What energy comes OUT?

AMore wind
BHeat
CElectrical energy
🌬️ Right! A wind turbine converts the motion of wind into electrical energy.
A wind turbine's job is to convert wind's motion into ELECTRICAL energy. A tiny bit becomes heat in the bearings, but the useful output is electricity.
Q3

A toaster also makes a SMALL amount of sound (the click when toast pops up). What is this sound called in the energy story?

AExtra output that wasn't the useful goal
BThe useful output
CThe input
⭐ Right! The toaster's useful output is heat. The click sound is extra — it wasn't the energy form you were trying to make.
The useful output of a toaster is HEAT (for warming bread). The click sound is extra — energy that comes out but isn't the form you wanted. No device is 100% efficient.
