📘 The Lesson: Patterns of Growth
Different cycles, same big idea
⏱ 15 min

Part 1 · Every Living Thing Has a Life Cycle

Today we look at the whole picture: every living thing goes through a life cycleLife cycleThe whole path from birth to making the next generation.. It starts with an egg, a seed or a tiny baby, and ends with that living thing making offspringOffspringThe new generation an organism makes. of its own. Then the cycle starts over.

Plant cycles look different from animal cycles, but the big steps are similar: start → grow → make the next generation. A sunflower seed grows into a seedling, then a flowering plant that makes new seeds. A monarch hatches from an egg, eats, changes its body, and becomes a butterfly that lays new eggs.

🛑
Stop & Think
"Pick any Florida plant or animal you know. What's its very first stage? What's its last stage before it makes offspring of its own?"

Part 2 · Two Kinds of Metamorphosis

Insects in Florida go through two main kinds of life cycles. The kind they go through is called metamorphosisMetamorphosisA big change in an animal's body during its life cycle.. It comes from a Greek word that means "change of shape."

  • CompleteComplete metamorphosisFour very different stages: egg, larva, pupa, adult. metamorphosis has four stagesStageOne step in a life cycle.: egg → larva → pupa (a resting stage, like a chrysalis) → adult. The young (larva) looks completely different from the adult. Butterflies, bees, and beetles all go through complete metamorphosis.
  • IncompleteIncomplete metamorphosisThree stages: egg, nymph, adult. metamorphosis has three stages: egg → nymph → adult. The young (nymph) looks like a smaller version of the adult and grows bigger as it sheds its skin. Grasshoppers, dragonflies, and cockroaches go through incomplete metamorphosis.

Birds, mammals, and reptiles don't go through metamorphosis at all. Their babies look like smaller versions of the adults from the start. A Florida bear cub looks like a tiny bear, not a different animal.

Watch out: Some people think "metamorphosis" means any kind of growing up. It really means a big change in body shape, like a caterpillar turning into a butterfly. A puppy growing into a dog is just growth, not metamorphosis.

Quick Self-Check
Not graded — just for you!
Q1

Put the stages of a monarch butterfly's life cycle in the right order.

ACaterpillar → egg → butterfly → chrysalis
BEgg → caterpillar → chrysalis → adult butterfly
CAdult butterfly → chrysalis → egg → caterpillar
🦋 Right! It starts with the egg, hatches into a caterpillar, becomes a chrysalis, and emerges as an adult.
Every life cycle starts at the beginning of the new generation. For a monarch, that's the egg, then caterpillar, then chrysalis, then adult.
Q2

What's the BIGGEST difference between complete and incomplete metamorphosis?

AComplete metamorphosis has a chrysalis or pupa stage; incomplete does not
BIncomplete metamorphosis has more stages than complete
COnly complete metamorphosis starts with an egg
🐛 Right! The big difference is the pupa/chrysalis stage where the body completely changes shape.
Look again: complete has FOUR stages (with a pupa); incomplete has THREE stages (no pupa). Both start with an egg.
Q3

A Florida bear cub looks like a tiny bear. A monarch caterpillar looks nothing like a butterfly. Why?

AThe bear is invasive and the butterfly is not
BThe bear is younger
CMammals don't go through metamorphosis; butterflies do (complete metamorphosis)
🦋 Right! Mammals grow without changing body shape. Butterflies go through complete metamorphosis, which means the caterpillar and adult look totally different.
The bear and butterfly are both native Florida animals. The real difference is that mammals don't go through metamorphosis — but butterflies do (complete metamorphosis).
