From Dr. Lena Reyes, field biologist

Will the chicks have it?

A wood stork parent came into our Field Station for a check-up. It had gotten tangled in fishing line near its nest. Its three chicks are safe back at the nest.

A volunteer filled in the stork’s traits, but left the last column blank: Will the chicks have it? She wasn’t sure how to tell. Can you help? First, practice on three animal families. Then finish my form.

VR Field Mission · about 12 minutes

Set up a family reunion

Build three animal families, each a parent and a baby. Then tell what the baby got from its parent, and what it won’t get.

0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your families
    Each family is two copies of the same model: a big one for the parent and a small one for the baby. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    Family 1Bear (OptimaED) + a small bear cubtype: Bear👪 cub got: black fur
    🌍 mom’s: ear tag from scientists
    Family 2Animated Deer + a small fawntype: Deer👪 fawn got: big ears
    🌍 mom’s: scrape from a fence
    Family 3Barn Owl + a small owlettype: Barn Owl👪 owlet got: heart-shaped face
    🌍 dad’s: knows where mice hide
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find The Countryside. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. Look around and pick three open spots for your families.
  5. Step 3 · Make the first family
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Bring out a Bear for the parent. Bring out a second Bear, then make it small. That’s the cub! Set the cub right next to its mom.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers and bring your hands together to make it smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Finish the reunion
    Make the deer family and the barn owl family the same way, a few steps apart. Then walk to each family and say two things out loud, like this:
    🗣 “The cub got its black fur from its mom. That’s inherited. Mom’s ear tag came from scientists. That’s from the environment, so the cub won’t get it.”
    Use the examples in Step 1, or think of your own.
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyAdd a fourth family that lives in the countryside: try Dairy Cow, Sheep, Pig or Chicken. Say one trait the baby inherited and one trait that is both (like size). Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see all three families. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 13.01 VR · Family Reunion. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one family and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

The pass-it-down gate

Your job1Send 10 traits through the gate→2Write a paragraph→3Upload your PDF

Here is every trait on the wood stork’s full chart. Decide where each one goes at the family gate.

  • ⬇ Passes to the chicks: the trait is inherited. The chicks will have it too.
  • ↕ Both: the parents pass down part of it, and the environment decides the rest.
  • ✋ Stays with the parent: it came from the environment. The chicks won’t get it.

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

Pick one trait the chicks will get and one trait they won’t get. Explain why each one does or does not pass down.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs trait inherited environment chicks or offspring because
Try these sentence starters:
  • The chicks will get the parent’s ___ because it is an inherited trait.
  • That means it came from ___.
  • The chicks won’t get the ___ because it came from the environment.
  • It happened when ___.
