Case File 15 · A washed-out map key
LR
From Dr. Lena Reyes, field biologist

Help me rebuild my map key!

Last night’s storm soaked my Florida resource map. The symbols made it, but the words in the map key washed right off.

A map without a key is just pretty dots. Which symbol is the citrus? Which one is the springs? Help me put the key back together, and make it big enough to walk through!

Build a resource museum

Make Dr. Reyes a walk-through map key: one exhibit for each Florida resource. Then give a tour.

0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your exhibits
    Your museum has four exhibits, one for each symbol in the key. Each exhibit sits on a Small Table. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    🍊 Citrus exhibitOptima Domi Orange, Orange Slicetype: OrangeFound in a wide band across central Florida. Orange trees need warm winters.
    🏖️ Beach exhibitConch Shell (Optima Domi), Seagulltype: Conch · SeagullFound all the way around the coast, where waves pile up sand.
    ⛏️ Phosphate exhibitPick Axe, Fossils · Vertebrae Fossil, Tomato Stage 4type: Pick Axe · Vertebrae · TomatoFound in two small clusters. Phosphate rock is full of old bones, and it makes plant food.
    💧 Springs exhibitWater Splash 1, Lilypads · White Lilytype: Splash · LilyFound bunched up in north central Florida, where limestone is close to the surface.
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Optima Gallery. (Typing Gallery in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. Find a long, open wall for your museum.
  5. Step 3 · Set up four stands
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Put four Small Tables in a row with space between them, like stops in a museum. Put each exhibit’s models on its own table.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Give a tour
    Walk your museum like a tour guide. At each exhibit, say what the resource is, what we use it for and where in Florida it is found, like this:
    🗣 “This is the springs exhibit. We swim and kayak in spring water, and it’s our drinking water too. Springs are bunched up in north central Florida.”
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyPut a Compass Rose on the floor at the museum door. Use it to tell a direction: “The springs are north of the citrus groves.” Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see all four exhibits. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 15.02 VR · Resource Museum. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one exhibit and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

How-to video: Completing Assignments in Optima On-Demand (2 minutes)

These pictures come from a space map. Your map will be Optima Gallery, but the buttons are the same.

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

  8. 8Pick the class and assignment

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.
