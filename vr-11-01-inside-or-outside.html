  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Tranquil Forest. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo.
  5. Step 3 · Bring out all 8
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu. Click the Models tab. In the Filter models box, type Frog Dissection. Click each part to bring it out.
    ✋ To move a part: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Make two piles
    Make an outside pile and an inside pile, with a big space between them. For each part, ask: could I see this on a frog sitting on a leaf?
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyLine up your inside pile from top to bottom, in the order the parts sit inside a frog’s body. Brain at the top! Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 11.01 VR · Inside or Outside? Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the parts and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Tell me you’re done
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. In the Canvas assignment box, type VR option submitted and turn it in.

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

Pick one outside part and one inside part of the frog. Tell what each part does to help the frog survive. Then explain how you knew which tray each part goes in.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs an outside part an inside part what a part does how you knew
Try these sentence starters:
  • One outside part of a frog is ___. It helps the frog ___.
  • One inside part of a frog is ___. Its job is to ___.
  • I knew ___ was outside because ___.
  • I knew ___ was inside because ___.
