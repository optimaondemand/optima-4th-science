Case File 12 · A scrambled radio log
LR
From Dr. Lena Reyes, field biologist

Static ate my messages!

Last night I followed a Florida panther with my field radio. I called in what it noticed and what it did. But static ate part of every message. Each one is missing steps.

Help me rebuild them. Every message needs all four steps:

⚡ Stimulus👂 Sense organ🧠 Brain🏃 Response
FIELD RADIO LOG PANTHER · NIGHT
9:04 PM“…dry leaves rustle… …panther creeps toward the sound.”
9:12 PM“…a tiny flash of light in the dark… ”
9:27 PM“ …its nose… …follows the trail.”
9:40 PM“ …a bite on its skin… ”
🥽
VR Field Mission · about 12 minutes

Build four sense stations

Put a panther in the middle. Around it, set up four stations, one for each thing it noticed last night.

0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your stations
    The Panther goes in the middle. Each station is one model: the thing the panther noticed. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    Station 1 · 9:04 PMA rat rustles the leavestype: Rat
    Station 2 · 9:12 PMA firefly flashestype: Firefly
    Station 3 · 9:27 PMA deer leaves a smell trailtype: Deer
    Station 4 · 9:40 PMA mosquito bitestype: Mosquito
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Explore The Brain. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. You’ll be standing inside a giant brain, the place where every message ends up!
  5. Step 3 · Place the panther
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Bring out the Panther and set it in an open spot. This is the middle of your stations.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Build the four stations
    Bring out the Rat, Firefly, Deer and Mosquito. Put each one a few steps away from the panther, in a circle around it. Then walk to each station and say its four steps out loud, like this:
    🗣 “The rat rustles the leaves (stimulus). The panther’s ears hear it (sense organ). Its brain decides, ‘Food!’ (brain). It creeps closer (response).”
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyAdd a fifth station of your own. Pick any model the panther could notice, put it in the circle, and say its four steps. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see the panther and all four stations. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 12.01 VR · Sense Stations. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one station and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

Pick one of your comic strips. Tell its four steps in order, using the step names. Then explain what would happen if the brain step went missing.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs stimulus sense organ brain response
Try these sentence starters:
  • The stimulus was ___.
  • The ___’s ___ (sense organ) picked it up.
  • Its brain decided ___, so its response was ___.
  • If the brain step was missing, ___.
⭐ Full points:
  • All 3 comic strips are finished.
  • Your paragraph is at least 40 words and all 4 checks are lit.
