  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know what to build
    Each model is an animal, the thing its body part acts on, and an arrow between them. In VR, the arrow is a model called Red Ray. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    Model 1 · talons grabHawk → snaketype: Eagle (our stand-in for a hawk), Snake, Red Ray
    Model 2 · tongue sipsBee → flowertype: Bee, Flower Set, Red Ray
    Model 3 · ears hear in the darkOwl → rattype: Barn Owl, Rat, Red Ray
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Optima Centum Gallery. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo.
  5. Step 3 · Build Model 1
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Bring out the Eagle and the Snake, with a little space between them. Then bring out a Red Ray and turn it so it points from the eagle’s feet to the snake. The point of the arrow touches the snake.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Build Models 2 and 3
    Build the bee → flower model and the owl → rat model the same way, each with its own Red Ray. Leave a big space between your three models, like exhibits in a museum.
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyInvent a fourth model: any animal, the thing its body part acts on, and a Red Ray between them. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 11.02 VR · Model Wall. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one of your models and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

How-to video: Completing Assignments in Optima On-Demand (2 minutes)

These pictures come from a space map. Your map will be Optima Centum Gallery, but the buttons are the same.

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

  8. 8Pick the class and assignment

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

Pick one of the osprey’s body parts. Tell what it does and which survival need it helps with. Then explain why your arrows turned the photo into a model, not just a picture.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs a body part what it does a survival need why it’s a model
Try these sentence starters:

  • This helps it get ___, which it needs to survive.
  • My arrows make it a model because ___.
