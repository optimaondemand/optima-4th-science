VR Field Mission · about 12 minutes

Grow two full circles

Grow a sunflower and a pumpkin from seed to seed. Then tell each plant’s story out loud.

0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your circles
    Each circle is six models in a ring, like the numbers on a clock. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    Circle 1 · SunflowerPlant Seeds, then Sunflower Stage 1, 2, 3 and 4type: Seeds · Sunflower🌱 start: the seeds
    🌻 end: the tall flower
    Circle 2 · PumpkinPlant Seeds, then Pumpkin Stage 1, 2, 3 and 4type: Seeds · Pumpkin🌱 start: the seeds
    🎃 end: the grown plant
    The pollinatorOne Bee for each circletype: Bee🐝 It goes between the last stage and the seeds. That closes the circle.
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Farm Town (Micro). (Typing Farm in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. Look around and pick two open spots for your circles.
  5. Step 3 · Grow the sunflower circle
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Bring out Plant Seeds and set it down. Then bring out Sunflower Stage 1, 2, 3 and 4, and set them around in a ring. Put a Bee after Stage 4, right next to the seeds, so the ring closes.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Grow the pumpkin circle
    A few steps away, make the pumpkin circle the same way: Plant Seeds, Pumpkin Stage 1 to 4, then a Bee. Then walk slowly around each circle and tell its story out loud, like this:
    🗣 “A seed sprouts and grows. The plant makes a flower. A bee carries pollen to the flower. The flower grows a fruit with new seeds inside. Back to seeds!”
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyShow how a seed can travel to a new spot. Bring out a Crow and an Apple and set them by one of your circles. Say out loud how the bird helps the apple’s seeds move. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see both circles. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 13.02 VR · Seed to Seed. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one circle and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

How-to video: Completing Assignments in Optima On-Demand (2 minutes)

These pictures come from a space map. Your map will be Farm Town (Micro), but the buttons are the same.

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

  8. 8Pick the class and assignment

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

Pick one fruit and tell its whole story: from a flower, to a fruit, to a new plant.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs flower pollen fruit seed because
Try these sentence starters:
  • My ___ started as a yellow flower.
  • A bee carried ___ to the flower.
  • Then the bottom of the flower grew into ___.
  • Inside were seeds, because ___.
  • If I plant one seed, it will grow into ___.
⭐ Full points:
  • All 3 fruits are grown, sliced and answered, and every fruit is matched to its flower.
  • Your paragraph is at least 40 words and all 5 checks are lit.
