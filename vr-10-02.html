Is the forest across the creek still in balance?

Last year, wild hogs moved into the forest across the creek from the old oak. I made two data sheets, one for each patch, but they’re still blank. Scientists don’t guess. They count.

Count it, don’t feel it: How many kinds of living things live in each plot? How many of each kind?

0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know what to bring
    You’ll build two plots (patches of forest). Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    🌿 Plot A · healthyAt least 5 different kinds, 1 or 2 of eachDeer, Rat, Grasshopper, Bee, Barn Owl, Ladybug, Mole, Fern, Flower Set
    🐗 Plot B · out of balanceAt least 5 wild hogs and only 1 other kindtype: Wild Boar (a wild hog), plus one Grasshopper
    🍄 Bonus · Plot AA decomposer by the rootstype: Fungal Cell or Bacteria
    🦋 The real plants and butterflies in the forest count too!
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Tranquil Forest. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo.
  5. Step 3 · Build Plot A (healthy)
    Pick a big tree. Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Around that tree, place at least 5 different kinds of living things, 1 or 2 of each.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Build Plot B (out of balance)
    Teleport to a different tree, far away from Plot A. Around it, place at least 5 Wild Boar and only 1 other kind of living thing, like one Grasshopper. The hogs have taken over!
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyGo back to Plot A and put a decomposer (a Fungal Cell or Bacteria) on the ground by the roots. Decomposers are part of a healthy forest too. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 10.02 VR · Two Plots. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Tell me you’re done
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. In the Canvas assignment box, type VR option submitted and turn it in.

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.

Why wild hogs? Wild hogs are not native to Florida. Spanish explorers brought pigs here about 500 years ago, and today wild hogs live in every Florida county. They dig up the ground with their snouts, which tears up plants and eats food that native animals need.
