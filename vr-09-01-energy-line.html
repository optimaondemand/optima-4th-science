0 of 7 steps

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know what to find
    Look at the 5 models you’ll need. Each one has its own search word. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    SunSearch Sun → pick Sun (Large)
    SunflowerSearch Sunflower → pick Sunflower Stage 4
    GrasshopperSearch Grasshopper → pick Grasshopper
    FrogSearch Frog → pick Frog
    SnakeSearch Snake → pick Snake
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find The Everglades. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo.
  5. Step 3 · Bring out all 5
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu. Click the Models tab. Type the first search word in the Filter models box and click the model to bring it out. Do the same for all 5.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller. The Sun is huge, so shrink it first!
  6. Step 4 · Build the energy line
    Put the 5 models in one straight line, in the order the energy moves. Start with the Sun. Leave a big step of space between each one. Ask yourself for each model: what does this one eat?
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyAdd a decomposer (a living thing that breaks down dead plants and animals). Search Bacteria, pick Bacteria 1, and put it just below the middle of your line. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 9.01 VR · Follow the Sun’s Energy. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Tell me you’re done
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. In the Canvas assignment box, type VR option submitted and turn it in.

So the hawk’s energy came from the Sun, five steps back.
Field Station stamp
Why does it start with the Sun? Animals can’t make their own food. Plants can: they use sunlight to make it. So every animal’s energy traces back to a plant, and every plant’s energy traces back to the Sun.

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

The hawk never eats plants. So where did its energy really start? Use at least 3 links of your energy line, and use the words producer and consumer.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs the Sun a producer a consumer how energy moves
Try these sentence starters:
  • The hawk’s energy started in the ___.
  • The sunflower is a producer because ___.
  • The grasshopper is a consumer because it eats ___.
  • When the ___ ate the ___, the energy moved to ___.
