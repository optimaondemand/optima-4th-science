📘 The Lesson: Air and Water Carry Energy
Moving air and water can push
⏱ 15 min

Part 1 · Moving Air = Wind Energy

Air is real stuff, even though you can't see it. When air moves, it carries motionMotionMovement. energy. That moving air is what we call WIND.

When wind energyWind energyEnergy of moving air. hits the blades of a pinwheel or a wind turbine, the air pushes on the blades. The blades start spinning. That spinning is energy being TRANSFERRED (passed along) from the moving air to the wheel. The wheel can then turn other things, like a pump or a generator (a machine that makes electricity).

Watch out: some people think air is nothing, because we can’t see it. Air is real stuff, and moving air can push. Try the drop race.

Part 2 · Moving Water = Water Energy

Falling water has motion energy too. So does flowing water in a river. When that water energyWater energyEnergy of moving water. hits the cups of a water wheel, it pushes them, and the wheel spins. Turn the valve and see.

  • Faster wind → bigger push → faster pinwheel spin.
  • More water → bigger push → faster waterwheel spin.
  • A modelModelA small version used to investigate. like a pinwheel helps you see what real wind turbines do.
  • Wind and water are renewableRenewableDoesn't run out.. Using them doesn't use them up.

How does Florida use them? Sailboats ride the wind, and old farm windmills pumped water for cattle. Florida has almost no wind farms, because its usual winds are gentle. But one dam on the Apalachicola River, at the Georgia line, uses moving water to make electricity.

Quick Self-Check
Not graded — just for you!
Q1

A pinwheel spins faster when the wind blows harder. Why?

AThe pinwheel makes its own energy
BStronger wind transfers MORE motion energy to the blades
CThe pinwheel gets lighter when wind blows
💨 Right! Stronger wind = bigger push on the blades = faster spin. The energy moves from the air into the wheel.
Pinwheels don't make their own energy. They receive energy from the moving air. A stronger wind pushes harder, so the wheel spins faster.
Q2

Why are wind and water considered RENEWABLE energy sources?

AThey never need a wheel or turbine
BThey are the only energy sources Florida uses
CUsing them doesn't use them up
🍃 Right! Wind keeps blowing. Water keeps flowing. We can use them without using them up.
Renewable means it KEEPS coming back. The Sun drives wind and the water cycle, so we never 'run out' of either, no matter how much we use.
Q3

Which observation would be the BEST evidence that wind has more motion energy on a stormy day than a calm day?

AA flag flaps faster and harder when it's stormy
BThe sky is gray on a stormy day
CIt is wet on a stormy day
📋 Right! The harder a flag flaps, the more energy is being transferred from the air to the flag. That's a measurable observation.
A gray sky or wet ground doesn't show how much motion energy the wind has. The flag flapping is the best CLUE about wind energy.
