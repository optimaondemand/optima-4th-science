Case File 16 · A permit marked PENDING
JC
From June Calloway, geologist

Approved only IF…

The town council read every comment card about the Palmetto Springs quarry. Then they sent the permit back with a big red stamp: PENDING.

The quarry would give us road rock and jobs. But it sits right next to Cypress Slough, and our wells are only a mile away. The council will say yes only if the permit lists ways to protect them. Will you fill in the blanks?

⛏️ Benefit: what we gain⚠️ Cost: what we could lose🛡️ Strategy: a way to lower a cost

Tap the box next to each step when you finish it.

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your model
    Your model has three places and at least two protections. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    ⛏️ The dig zoneGranite Block (two of them), Pick Axe, Cargo Trucktype: Granite · Pick Axe · Cargo TruckBenefit: rock for roads and buildings, and jobs.
    🌿 Cypress SloughFern, Lilypads · White Lily, Crowtype: Fern · Lily · CrowCost: birds and plants could lose their home.
    💧 The town wellsWater Barrel (two of them)type: Water BarrelCost: digging could dirty the water under the ground.
    🛡️ Protections (pick 2 or more)Stone Planter 1x4m (a plant buffer), Chem · Test Tube Rack (test the water), Plant Seeds (replant later)type: Planter · Test Tube · SeedsA strategy lowers a cost. It doesn’t make it zero.
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find OptimaED Science Lab. (Typing Lab in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. Find a big lab table. (No table? Build on the floor.)
  5. Step 3 · Build it small
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Shrink each model so it fits on the table. Put the dig zone on one end and Cypress Slough on the other end. Put the wells off to the side, a little farther away.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Add protections and explain
    Put at least two protections where they help: the planter between the pit and the slough, the test tubes by the wells, the seeds in the pit for later. Then point to each part and say it out loud:
    🗣 “The quarry gives us road rock. It could hurt the wetland. The plant buffer keeps the dust and digging away from the birds.”
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyBuild an “after” corner: what the pit could become when the digging is done. Try Lilypads · Large, Flower Set 1 and a Bench. Say which strategy it shows. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see the whole table. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 16.01 VR · Quarry Model. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

How-to video: Completing Assignments in Optima On-Demand (2 minutes)

These pictures come from a space map. Your map will be OptimaED Science Lab, but the buttons are the same.

  1. 1Explore tab

    On your dashboard, click the Explore tab. Scroll with the bar on the right, or type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 2Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo. Only a solo room (just you) can save your work.

  3. 3Open your menu

    Press the top button on your right controller. The menu tabs are Environments, Models, Assignments and Captures.

  4. 4Read the assignment

    Click Assignments, then click our assignment. What to build shows on the right.

  5. 5Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type in the Filter models box to find the parts. Click a part to bring it out.

  6. 6Move and resize

    Grab a part with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  7. 7Capture

    When it looks right, open your menu, click Captures, then click Capture.

  8. 8Pick the class and assignment

    Type a title. For Class, pick our class, not No class (personal). For Assignment, pick ours. Click Save.

  9. 9Check, then leave

    Look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu. Then click Leave in the top right corner.

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

How would you vote on the quarry? Name one benefit and one cost. Then tell which protection matters most to you, and why.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs your vote a benefit a cost a protection because
Try these sentence starters:
  • I would vote ___ on the quarry.
  • One benefit is ___.
  • One cost is ___.
  • The protection that matters most to me is ___ because ___.
