Case File 9 · Silent night
LR
From Dr. Lena Reyes, field biologist

No frogs. Is the hawk in trouble?

After the dry spell, the green tree frogs left the marsh at post 7. The volunteer pinned this note to my case file:

“No frogs? Then the hawk will starve.”— the volunteer

In the lesson, you saw our hawk eat a snake. So it eats more than frogs! But what else is out here for it to eat? I need a field survey: a careful look at every living thing in one place.

Your rule from the lesson: a food web has backup paths. An animal that eats many things can switch to other food when one food disappears.
🐸 frogs: gone🦅 hawk’s backup food: you find it!

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know what to look for
    Find at least 5 living things: at least 1 plant and at least 3 animals. Try to remember where each one was and what it was doing.
    🌿Plantsproducers
    🐇Animals that eat plants
    🦎Small animals a hawk could catch
    💧Living things in or near the water
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Tranquil Forest. (Typing it in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo.
  5. Step 3 · Walk and look
    Use your thumbstick to teleport (jump) to a new spot. At each spot, turn slowly in a full circle. Look up in the trees and sky, down at the ground, and into the water. Visit at least 3 spots.
  6. Step 4 · Think like the hawk
    For every animal you see, ask: could a hawk catch this one? And for every plant: who eats this?
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyStand still, close your eyes and listen for 30 seconds. What do you hear? You’ll write one sound in your field notes. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Leave
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu. Click Leave in the top right corner and take off your headset. There’s nothing to capture today.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Write it down fast
    Fill in your field notes below right away, while you still remember what you saw.

    Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

    Was the volunteer right? Will the hawk starve without frogs? Use at least 2 living things from your field notes as evidence. Then tell one thing that could change in the marsh now that the frogs are gone.

    0 of 40 words

    • The hawk is part of a food web, so ___.
    • Without frogs, there might be more ___ because ___.
