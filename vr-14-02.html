Where did the robin go?

Last summer, our Field Station put a tiny metal band on a young robin’s leg in Maine. This January, it turned up in Tallahassee, Florida, eating holly berries!

When the seasons change, everything changes: who travels, who sleeps, who stays, and what the plants are doing. Let’s look at a whole year.

✈️ Some travel😴 Some sleep🧥 Some stay and tough it out

  1. 🖥️Before you put on your headset
  2. Step 1 · Know your wheel
    An Analog Clock goes in the middle. Four stations go around it, like the numbers on the clock. Type the gray words in the Filter models box. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click Assignments, then click our assignment.
    ❄️ Winter · at 12 o’clockSnowfall Effect, Thermometer - 31F, Beartype: Snow · Thermometer · BearThe bear sleeps in its den and lives on its fat.
    🌷 Spring · at 3 o’clockRain Effect, Yellow Tulip Stage 3, Bee, Frogtype: Rain · Tulip · Bee · FrogThe bee needs the flowers for food. The flowers need the bee to carry pollen.
    ☀️ Summer · at 6 o’clockThermometer - 95F, Sunflower Stage 4, Monarch Butterfly, Dragonflytype: Thermometer · Sunflower · Monarch · DragonflyLots of food: the monarch sips nectar, the dragonfly catches bugs.
    🍂 Fall · at 9 o’clockPumpkin Stage 4, Wheat Stage 4, Crowtype: Pumpkin · Wheat · CrowThe crow eats ripe seeds to store fat for winter. The monarch flies south.
  3. 🥽In VR
  4. Step 2 · Go in
    Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab and find Classical Outdoor Classroom. (Typing Classroom in the Filter environments box finds it fast.) Click it, then click Begin Solo. Find a big open spot for your wheel.
  5. Step 3 · Start the wheel
    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu, then click the Models tab. Set the Analog Clock in the middle. Build winter at 12 o’clock and spring at 3 o’clock, a few steps out from the clock.
    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Grab it with both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  6. Step 4 · Finish the year
    Build summer at 6 o’clock and fall at 9 o’clock. Then walk around the clock, season by season, and say one way living things need each other or change, like this:
    🗣 “In spring, the bee needs the tulips for food, and the tulips need the bee to carry their pollen.” · “In winter, the bear sleeps, because there is almost no food.”
  7. Step 5 · Bonus
    VR onlyWhere do the travelers go? Outside your wheel, build a little Florida winter spot: a Thermometer - 72F, a Monarch Butterfly and a Seagull. Say why some animals come to Florida for winter. Skip this step if you run out of time.
  8. Step 6 · Save (capture)
    Stand back so you can see the whole wheel. Open your menu and click the Captures tab, then click Capture. Type a title, like your first name. For Class, pick our class (not No class (personal)). For Assignment, pick 14.02 VR · Season Wheel. Click Save, then look for Submitted at the bottom of the menu.
    🔁 Want to fix something? Move the models and save again. Your new capture takes the place of the old one.
  9. ✅After
  10. Step 7 · Add your caption in Canvas
    Click Leave in the top right corner of your menu and take off your headset. VR can’t hold words, so your caption goes in Canvas. Pick one season and finish this sentence:
    Then click the green button below the steps when it appears, and paste into the Canvas assignment box. (Or type VR option submitted and your caption yourself.)

Explain your thinking

Write a paragraph (4 or 5 sentences)

Pick one bird. Tell where it spent the winter, why it went (or stayed), and what it needed to survive.

0 of 40 words
Your paragraph needs winter migrate or stay food a place because
Try these sentence starters:
  • The ___ was banded in ___ in summer.
  • In winter, it was found in ___.
  • It went there because ___.
  • In winter up north, ___.
  • To survive, it needed ___.
